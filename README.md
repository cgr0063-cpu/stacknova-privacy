# Privacy Policy for StackNova

Last updated: October 5, 2026

StackNova is a mobile block puzzle game developed for entertainment purposes.

## Information We Collect

StackNova does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or address.

The game does not intentionally collect, store, or share personal or sensitive user data.

## Game Data

Game progress, settings, scores, or similar gameplay information may be stored locally on the user's device when necessary for the operation of the game.

This information is not used to personally identify users.

## Internet and Third-Party Services

StackNova may use platform or technical services necessary for distributing and operating the application. These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## Advertising

StackNova currently does not display third-party advertisements.

## In-App Purchases

StackNova currently does not offer in-app purchases.

## Children's Privacy

StackNova does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Sharing

StackNova does not sell or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. Since StackNova does not intentionally collect personal information, no personal user database is maintained by the game.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its features change. Any updates will be published on this page.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the StackNova Google Play listing.
